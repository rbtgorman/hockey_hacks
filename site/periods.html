How to read this

At 10 to 13 games, luck explains almost all of the spread between teams on these measures. A team that is exactly league average lands anywhere from about 25% to 80% over 10 games. That is why the shrunk column barely moves from the norm: the flatness is the finding.

Regulation only
Periods 1–3. Overtime and shootout goals never count.
Empty-net goals
They count. One measure removes them from the 3rd period, where they are about 17% of goals (2022-26).
First 10 minutes
A goal by either team before 10:00 of the 1st period.
Last 10
Each team's ten most recent games.
95% range
The Wilson interval for the team's rate: where a team with that true rate would land 95% of the time over the same number of games.
Shrunk
The team's record pulled toward the norm: (hits + k × norm) ÷ (games + k). k comes from four seasons of team records, using Tango's regression to the mean.
xG expected
What the shots deserved. Each game's actual unblocked shots, with xG from the model, treated as independent chances, then scaled to the measure's 2022-26 league ratio. Ties get no xG estimate, because treating shots as independent misses them by about 7 points.

The list of measures follows the period charts at Otto's Hockey Portal (@PsuOtto). Every number here is computed from NHL play-by-play by this project.
