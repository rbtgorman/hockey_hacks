How to read this

Even over a full 82-game season, luck explains most of the difference between teams on these stats. A team that's dead-on league average can land anywhere from about 25% to 80% over 10 games. That's why the Shrunk column barely budges from the norm.

Regulation only
Periods 1 through 3. OT and shootout goals don't count.
Empty-net goals
They count. One stat takes them out of the 3rd, where they're about 17% of goals (2022-26).
First 10 minutes
A goal by either team before 10:00 of the 1st period.
Last 10
Each team's last ten games.
League norm
How often it happened across every regular-season game from 2022-23 through 2025-26.
