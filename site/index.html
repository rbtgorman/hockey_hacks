Why there aren't any picks or projections yet

Baseball's got it easy. MLB puts pitch-by-pitch tracking data out there for anyone, and every team plays 162 games. Hockey doesn't give you nearly that much. The NHL puts out the play-by-play, including where every shot came from, but its player-tracking numbers (NHL EDGE) only come out as summaries.

And 82 games isn't a lot. If you've played, you know how it goes: some nights every bounce goes your way, some nights you can't buy a goal. Over a season, that puck luck hides how good a team really is.

So I'm building this one piece at a time, and nothing gets stacked on a piece that hasn't been tested.

How much puck luck are we talking? Take "2+ goals in the 1st period." A team needs about 356 games, more than four full seasons, before its own record tells you as much as the league average does. See it for every stat

How it's getting built

  1. 1
    Get the data
    Every NHL game since 2022-23, straight from the NHL's free public feed: play-by-play, shifts and box scores. That's 5,794 games and 499,443 shots that weren't blocked, through October 28, 2026, and it keeps updating during the season.
    Done
  2. 2
    Figure out shot quality
    A model looks at every shot that wasn't blocked and says how likely it was to go in. That's expected goals, or xG. It works, and you can see how well below. What it doesn't know yet is who took the shot or who was in net.
    In progress
  3. 3
    Size up teams fairly
    Period scoring: how often each team's games hit stuff like 2+ goals in the 1st, next to how much of that could just be luck and what the shots say should've happened.
    In progress
  4. 4
    Simulate games
    Not yet. First the model needs to know the shooters and the goalies, and it needs more seasons under the way the NHL tracks shots now. Nothing gets posted as a projection until it's been tested on a season it never saw.
    Not started

So is the shot model any good?

I tested it on the whole 2025-26 season: 118,605 unblocked shots it had never seen. It learned from 2022-23 through 2024-25.

Something I found along the way

My first models only learned from shots on goal, and every one of them came in 7–8% short on goals in 2024-25. Turns out scoring hadn't really gone up. Goals per shot on goal went up, but goals per unblocked shot barely moved. That's what you'd see if some saves were getting written down as missed shots, and David Johnson at Hockey Analysis thinks puck tracking might be doing exactly that. Once I counted every unblocked shot (goals, saves and misses together), the miss dropped to 3%. That's why everything on this site uses unblocked shots.

The full write-up is on GitHub

Where it still struggles

Goals scored for every 100 the model expected in 2025-26. 100 means it's right on.

  • Tip-ins: 78. It thinks tips go in more than they do.
  • Shots from 60+ feet: 88. Long bombs go in less than it thinks.
  • Power play: 94. It's a little too high on PP shots.
  • Who's shooting, who's in net. It doesn't know yet, so a top-line sniper and a fourth-line grinder get the same xG for the same shot.
